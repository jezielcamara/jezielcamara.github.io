04 / FIELD NOTES

How I look at business websites.

01 What should a homepage explain in 10 seconds? +

What the business does, who it helps, and the next action a visitor should take. Everything else supports those three things.

02 Why does mobile deserve its own decisions? +

A phone layout should not be a squeezed desktop page. Navigation, image order, spacing, and contact actions need to become simpler.

03 What would I fix first on an outdated company site? +

I would simplify the first screen, clarify the services, remove dead ends, and make the contact path visible without hunting for it.
